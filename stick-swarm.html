About Stick Swarm

Stick Swarm is a fast stickman survival game. Hordes of enemies close in from every side. Defeat them to gain experience, level up, and pick weapons and upgrades that make your stickman stronger every run.

Weapons include orbiting sticks that slice through swarms and huge energy arcs. Stomp from the air, double jump, unlock heroes and skins, take on daily challenges, and climb the leaderboard. Can you defeat the final boss, The Eraser?

Controls

  • Desktop: A/D or arrow keys to move, W or Space to jump (double jump in the air), S in the air to stomp, Shift or K to dash, R or E for Swarm Burst, Esc or P to pause.
  • Phone and tablet: use the on-screen pad to move, and the JUMP, DASH and ULT buttons. Swipe down on the pad to drop or stomp.

FAQ
