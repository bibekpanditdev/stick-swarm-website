About Stick Swarm

Stick Swarm is a fast, fast-paced stickman survival game you can play free online. Hordes of enemies close in from every side while you fight to survive. Defeat them to collect experience, level up, and choose new weapons and passive upgrades that make your stickman stronger every run.

Weapons include orbiting sticks that slice through swarms and huge 360-degree energy arcs. Between runs you can spend rewards in the upgrade shop, try daily challenges, climb the leaderboard and unlock achievements. Can you survive long enough to defeat the final boss, The Eraser?

How to Play

1. Move & Survive

Move your stickman around the arena to dodge enemies. On mobile use the on-screen touch controls; on desktop use your keyboard.

2. Collect & Level Up

Defeat enemies to gain experience. Each level up lets you pick a new weapon or passive upgrade.

3. Upgrade Between Runs

Earn rewards and spend them in the shop to start every new run stronger.

4. Beat the Boss

Survive the waves and defeat The Eraser to conquer the swarm.

Game Features

  • Endless enemy swarms and boss fights
  • Multiple weapons, passives and characters
  • Permanent upgrade shop
  • Daily challenges and leaderboard
  • Achievements to unlock
  • Plays instantly on desktop and mobile

Tips & Tricks

  • Keep moving. Standing still gets you surrounded fast.
  • Pick weapons that work well together instead of spreading upgrades thin.
  • Spend early rewards in the shop to boost survivability.
  • Fight near the edge of a crowd so you always have an escape route.

FAQ

Is Stick Swarm free to play?

Yes, it is 100% free in your browser, with no download or signup.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. Touch controls are built in and it runs on phones and tablets.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in any modern browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

Is my progress saved?

Progress is stored in your browser. Keep using the same browser and avoid clearing site data.

The game does not load. What should I do?

Use an up-to-date browser, check your connection, and refresh. The first load downloads about 15 MB.
